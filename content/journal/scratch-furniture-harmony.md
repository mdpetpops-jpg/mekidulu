## Redefining Feline Home Harmony

*Scratching is not a destructive habit; it is a biological requirement. The key to furniture preservation is redirection, not restriction.*

> “Design is not just what it looks like; it's how it preserves the harmony of a modern home.” — Mekidulu Design Lab

### The 10 Strategic Insights (Q&A)

**Q1. Why do cats prefer my luxury sofa?**
It's about "Resistance." Sofas provide a stable counter-force. Mekidulu uses FAS-grade wood to offer that same structural stability.

**Q2. The "Vertical Stretch" requirement**
Cats need to fully extend their spine to relieve tension. If a post is too short, they will look for the nearest tall object.

**Q3. High-density sisal vs. Fabric**
High-density sisal mimics tree bark, offering a superior "shred-feel" that redirects instinctual drive away from fabric upholstery.

**Q4. Strategic Placement**
Cats mark territory via scent glands in paws. Placing a post near a "high-traffic" corner creates a new, acceptable marking zone.

**Q5. Design aesthetics & acceptance**
A beautiful piece won't be hidden in a backroom. Better visibility leads to more frequent use by the cat.

**Q6. Rock-Solid engineering**
Wobbly posts feel unsafe. Our 10 × 10 cm core columns ensure zero-vibration, making it the cat's first choice every time.

**Q7. Hygiene & Solid Wood**
Unlike carpet-covered trees, solid wood is easy to sanitize, reducing allergens in the living room environment.

**Q8. Air Quality Standards**
Using zero-formaldehyde birch prevents chemical off-gassing, ensuring a healthy environment for both pets and humans.

**Q9. The Modular Investment**
Instead of replacing a sofa, owners can simply replace the sisal sleeve, making it a sustainable, long-term furniture insurance.

**Q10. Luxury vs. Necessity**
It is an insurance policy. The cost of a Mekidulu masterpiece is far less than reupholstering a designer sofa.
